# AvvA VISUAL GRAMMAR

SPACE HAS FUNCTION.

The semantic relationship and the visual composition are separate layers.

## 01 / SEMANTIC FORM

`text
Dawa <──── AvvA ────> Avalhla
`

Meaning:

`text
Dawa  = human choice
AvvA  = relation / living threshold
Ava   = CrossWorld aspect reference when scoped to this repository
`

## 02 / SPINE GEOMETRY

The canonical wordmark uses one fixed vertical spine.

The geometry is intentionally landmark-based, not ordinary word-centering.

`text
                 Dawa
                   │
              > AvvA <
                   │
              Avalhla
`

Landmarks:

- TOP: the **D** of `Dawa` anchors the spine.
- MIDDLE: the **vv** pair of `AvvA` is the true center.
- BOTTOM: the **second l** in `Avalhla` is the spine landmark.

The line is the joining axis.

## 03 / NON-RULES

Do not center all three words conventionally.

Do not alter spelling to make the geometry symmetrical.

Do not turn the visual spine into a semantic identity rule.

Do not let terminal font variation redefine the canonical relation.

## 04 / SURVIVAL LAW

`text
THE CHARACTER IS NOT THE ART.
THE GRID IS NOT THE MEANING.
THE FACE IS NOT THE IDENTITY.
THE COMPOSITION CARRIES THE STORY.
THE RELATION SURVIVES THE SKIN.
`

The renderer may provide implementation details such as fixed columns or font assumptions, while this grammar preserves the intended composition.
