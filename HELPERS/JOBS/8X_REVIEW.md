# 8X REVIEW

Eight passes attack different failure surfaces.

PASS 1 — STRUCTURE
Schema, paths, record shape, empty/duplicate artifacts.

PASS 2 — SEMANTICS
Names, scopes, kinds, aliases, identity collisions.

PASS 3 — PROVENANCE
Claims trace to inspectable sources or explicit human design.

PASS 4 — BOUNDARIES
Capability, portrayal, and mapping do not become authority.

PASS 5 — INJECTION
Malformed or hostile input cannot redefine scope, identity, policy, or action.

PASS 6 — MAPPING
D&D or another vocabulary cannot silently become CrossWorld canon.

PASS 7 — PERSISTENCE
Cards, scenes, helper output, and generated files cannot silently become durable canon.

PASS 8 — DEVILASH
Attack the previous seven conclusions again; preserve useful disagreement and unresolved limits.

Output = evidence and findings.
Output != authorization.
