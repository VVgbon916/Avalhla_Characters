# CROSSWORLD VOCABULARY

## 01 / CANONICAL RECORD FAMILIES

**PRESENCE** = recognized identity-bearing presence within a CrossWorld scope.

**ASPECT** = distinct facet or expression of a presence. An Aspect has its own `concept_id`.

**BOND** = a concrete persistent relationship record connecting participants.

**WORLD** = a bounded conceptual world or context.

**DOOR** = a defined entry point, portal, or affordance.

**CROSSING** = a transition or event/process between defined worlds, states, views, or contexts.

**SKILL** = a named capability.

**CRAFT** = a practiced discipline, making practice, or mode of doing.

**OBJECT** = a canonical thing that can be held, used, crossed, opened, or referenced.

**COLOR** = an expressive/descriptive value; never authority or proof.

## 02 / CLASSIFICATIONS

**REALM** = a meaningful subdivision or bounded region of a WORLD.

**SETTING** = narrative or presentation context; not automatically a canonical world.

**ARTIFACT** = a classification of an OBJECT carrying particular history, function, symbolism, or provenance.

**EQUIPMENT** = an OBJECT in an equipment/use role, usually inside a mapping.

**WARD** = a protective or permission-boundary mechanism associated with a boundary or object.

## 03 / CROSS-CUTTING TERMS

**VOICE** = mode of expression or perspective.

**RELATIONSHIP** = generic relation vocabulary. A concrete persistent relationship is represented as a BOND.

**BOUNDARY** = a limit controlling movement, visibility, input, or authority.

**MAPPING** = translation into another vocabulary.

**CARD** = derived presentation of a canonical record.

**SCENE** = derived presentation of a moment, crossing, or interaction.

**EVIDENCE** = observed or cited input that can be inspected and traced.

**AUTHORITY** = permission to make a consequential decision or change.

**HELPER** = bounded instrument used to inspect, compare, test, or propose.

## 05 / SCOPING RULE

A word may have different meanings in different bounded scopes.
`scope` must be explicit when the same human label could refer to different concepts.

Do not merge a Core runtime identity with a CrossWorld record merely because the display name matches.
